Query Protocol [SYS.FAQ]

Standard responses for common operational queries regarding AI HUNTX.

[01] Who is authorized to compete in AI HUNTX? +

All registered students of Sathyabama Institute of Science and Technology across all engineering, computing, and management disciplines are authorized to form squads.

[02] What are the allowed squad capacity limits? +

Squads must configure between 2 and 5 members (1 squad leader + 1 to 4 tactical members). Registrations are hard-capped at 50 total squads.

[03] Is a pre-built neural prototype required prior to event? +

Negative. The problem statements and AI hunt challenges will be encrypted and released on-site during the morning mission briefing.

[04] What AI frameworks and tools are permissible? +

Participants may use any standard AI platforms, open-source LLMs, computer vision libraries, Python data science ecosystems, and automation APIs.

[05] Are there any registration fees or entry levies? +

Zero registration fees. Entry is 100% free of cost courtesy of The Robotics Society Sathyabama.

[06] How are squads notified of confirmation? +

Automatic confirmation telemetry is transmitted immediately to the leader's email upon successful authorization through this console.
